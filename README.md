# Love-Score-Detector
Detects the Love score between a couple or any two people.

**Input:
**
Any two names of two different persons

**Output:
**
Love Score out of 100

Inspired by:

[FLAMES]([url](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FLAMES_(game))) is a traditional game played in our school years. This is used to find the future relationship between two people. The relationship will be one of the following:

1. Friendship
2. Love
3. Affection
4. Marriage
5. Enemity
6. Sister/Brother

Though this game is played for fun, some people used to believe the result of this game.

This application is to share the relationship of the two people (names) provided.
